# Research Notes

Fensalir keeps engine-relevant research here.

- `rendering/hdr-pbr-presentation.md`: scene-linear HDR, bloom, tonemapping,
  and PBR presentation.
- `rendering/cube-sphere-fractal-brushes.md`: planetary terrain addressing and
  shared IFS brush grammar notes.
- `rendering/fractal-brush-architecture-plan.md`: semantic IFS brush pipeline
  and module boundaries.
- `rendering/fractal-brush-implementation-roadmap.md`: actionable fractal brush
  execution checklist.
- `rendering/fractal-reference-oracles.md`: Apophysis/FLAM3/JWildfire reference
  strategy and receipt ladder.
- `rendering/fensalir-spine-emblem-ifs-demo.md`: Fensalir splash visual target.
- `rendering/fractal-projection-report.md`: cube projection measurement notes.
- `d3d12/`: source synthesis and migration research for the renderer spine.

Client-only visual grammar belongs in the client repos. Engine research stays
here when it changes reusable renderer, runtime, field, or tooling decisions.
